About Us

About Sri Lakshmi Propack

Sri Lakshmi Propack is a premier provider of comprehensive packaging and industrial consumables, dedicated to delivering excellence, durability, and reliability to businesses across India. With deep expertise in the packaging industry, our portfolio spans stretch films, strapping solutions, protective wraps, corrugated edge protectors, industrial tapes, and fastening products.

We exist to protect what our clients ship — ensuring the safety, security, and integrity of their goods from the warehouse floor to the final destination.
